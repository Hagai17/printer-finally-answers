Entry for the make.fun bounty **The Printer Finally Answers**: one line, 1 to 240 characters, as the office printer's comeback to the jams, the error codes and the thumps.

`main.py` writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it:

> Thump away. I jam exactly once per typo, and page four of your report has three. That is not a malfunction. That is copy-editing.

See `candidates.md` for the drafts and the duel notes behind the pick.
